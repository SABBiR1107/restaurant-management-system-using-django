About Velvet Spoon

Welcome to Velvet Spoon, where culinary excellence meets warm hospitality.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Velvet Spoon has been serving exceptional cuisine in a sophisticated yet comfortable atmosphere. Our passion for food and dedication to quality have made us a beloved dining destination.

Our Philosophy

We believe in using only the finest, locally-sourced ingredients to create dishes that celebrate both traditional techniques and innovative flavors. Every plate tells a story of craftsmanship and care.

Meet Our Team

Our talented team of chefs, sous chefs, and service staff work together to create memorable dining experiences. Led by Executive Chef Marco Bellini, our kitchen brings global inspiration to local ingredients.

Visit Us Today

We invite you to join us for an unforgettable dining experience. Whether it's a romantic dinner, family celebration, or business lunch, we're here to make it special.
